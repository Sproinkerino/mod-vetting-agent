# reddit-pi Brief

## Project

**Name:** reddit-pi

**Description:** A mobile-first Reddit research tool that turns a comment URL or username into compact, source-linked evidence and copy-ready factual rebuttals. It serves people who want to challenge a public claim without inventing details or losing the original context.

**Target audience:** Reddit readers and participants who want fast receipts before replying.

**Business goal:** Make the cited result useful and shareable enough that each copied claim check introduces another person to reddit-pi.

## The four axes

### Tone Register: Provocative, disciplined

The voice has the energy of a sharp comeback while every claim stays tied to public evidence.

### Aesthetic Philosophy: Controlled maximalism

Dense navy surfaces, hot orange, ultraviolet accents, and expressive type create drama without sacrificing scanning speed.

### Audience Relationship: Capable sidekick

reddit-pi hands the user receipts and context; it does not pretend to be judge, moderator, or moral authority.

### Sensory Ambition: Resonant

Motion and contrast make investigation feel alive, while reduced-motion support and restrained effects keep the interface usable.

## Synthesis

This brief produces a compact evidence product, not an admin dashboard. The target comment and copy-ready response dominate; supporting quotes, behavioral patterns, and the full archive progressively disclose below. The design feels playful and internet-native, but its language remains exact and source-conscious.

## Inspiration references

- **Downloaded reddit-pi handoff** — establishes the navy, orange, purple, mobile shell, and comeback-first hierarchy.
- **hdb-engine** — calibrates dense information presentation and purposeful animated atmosphere.

## Rejection list

This brief explicitly says no to:

- No moderation workflow labels, pipeline warnings, or adjudication language in the primary experience.
- No oversized username or empty dashboard whitespace.
- No unsupported personality verdicts, sensitive-trait inference, or generated harassment.
- No fake percentage progress when the backend cannot provide real stage completion.
- No desktop-first layouts that collapse poorly on a phone.

## Open questions

- Whether a future backend should expose real named progress stages.
- Whether users should be able to save case files beyond the three-day analysis cache.
